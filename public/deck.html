How it works

Three steps, no training required

Step 01

Place a tag

Stick an NFC tag at the walk-in, the handwash sink, the fryer — wherever the task lives.

Step 02

Staff tap to complete

Employees tap their phone against the tag when the task is done. No app to dig for, no login to remember.

Step 03

Managers see it live

Every tap lands in a running log — who, where, and exactly when — visible from the console in real time.
